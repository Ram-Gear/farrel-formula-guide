About this guide

A read-only reference reproducing Roger Blake’s one-page “Explanation of the Farrel Formula” (Farrel manual #610A, pp. 45 & 46): the formula, how it applies to gearsets and individual gears, and which of his conversion programs to use for each kind of drawing. Use Print for a paper copy.

Abbreviations

TermStands for
CTTTCircular Tooth Thickness, Transverse
CTTTP / CTTTGCTTT of the Pinion / Gear
CTTN / CTTNP / CTTNGCircular Tooth Thickness, Normal (of Pinion / Gear)
CPTCircular Pitch, Transverse
CPNCircular Pitch, Normal
PD / PDEPitch Diameter / Pitch Diameter, Effective (operating)
PDPE / PDGEPDE of the Pinion / Gear
OD / ODP / ODGOutside Diameter (of Pinion / Gear)
DPTDiametral Pitch, Transverse
PATPressure Angle, Transverse
TANPATTangent of PAT
INVPAT / INVPATEInvolute of PAT / of the effective (operating) PAT
NNumber of teeth
XRadial offset from the comparator reading used in the formula
HAHelix Angle
CR / CRP / CRGComparator Reading (of Pinion / Gear)
BLBacklash
BLCT / BLCNBacklash, Circular, Transverse / Normal (BLCN assumed = feeler gage backlash)
C / CECenter distance / Center distance, Effective
RCRatio CE / C
B&S / BSBrown & Sharpe (chordal dimensions)
MOBMeasurement Over Balls
O / E (program names)Odd / Even number of teeth

Expansions are standard gear-shop usage (as used in the sibling Ram-Gear RC Blake apps); the source sheet itself does not spell them out.
